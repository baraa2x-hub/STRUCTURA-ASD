Splice Maker · new

Bar too long?
Splice it right.

ASD makes one locked bar when a bar is longer than stock. Splice Maker splits it into separate real bars that overlap by the lap: equal, at the middle, full stock or your own lengths. Each piece gets its own ASD description, all in one undo.

Lap = 60Ø or mmTouching or offsetReal ASD bars
